# Community conduct

Discuss ideas and technical evidence respectfully. Do not harass contributors, attack personal characteristics, publish private information, or pressure users to claim abilities or accessibility outcomes they have not experienced.

Accessibility feedback is substantive product evidence. Listen to disabled folders and preserve their consent and privacy in review records. Disagreement about geometry, instruction wording, naming or a design's origin should be resolved through reproducible examples and credible sources.

The hosting repository's maintainers may moderate discussion and restrict participation to protect contributors. Report concerns through the host's available private reporting tools; this initial draft does not invent a separate reporting service.
