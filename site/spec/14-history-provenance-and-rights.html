14 · History, provenance and rights

Normative data contract; editorial guidance is informative

metadata.sources contains stable IDs, titles, HTTPS URLs and optional access dates/rights notes. step.sourceSteps preserves source labels when a complex source instruction becomes several clearer teaching steps. A camera change, translation or prose rewrite must not silently remove provenance.

A model may carry history records with a localized title/summary, source IDs, qualifications and review. scope distinguishes model, symbol, and tradition:

  • Model history concerns the particular design or documented author.
  • Symbol history concerns meanings attached to the represented object, not proof of a design’s origin.
  • Tradition context concerns a broader practice associated with a place or community.

These scopes prevent a general cultural story being presented as the invention history of a specific modern pattern. The crane example uses symbol for a sourced peace-context note. It does not invent a designer or date for its digital sequence.

A history record must cite at least one existing source ID. Readers show sources/qualifications and can hide optional context. Applications should not automatically append Japanese origins to every paper animal or contemporary pattern. Missing evidence is a reason to omit a claim, not to create a generic legend.

Generic educational facts belong in an application content catalogue, not implicitly in every model. A reusable app fact can be shown beside a model with clear labeling, but it must not appear as that model’s exported historical claim without deliberate authorship and references.

Licensing follows the content: code, original instruction text, adapted prose, photographs, narration and printed paper images can have different rights. Preserve per-asset licenses and document rights notes. A source that says non-commercial use only must not be relabeled as unrestricted merely because the application code uses MIT.

This repository’s examples and prose use original explanatory wording; historical notes cite their factual sources. No external website’s complete instruction collection is mirrored here. An importer/collector is not part of the core file format, and retrieving a page does not establish permission to redistribute it.
