Your first fold
Document: first-fold / revision 1
Status: instructions

A small text-only lesson demonstrating clear copy, tactile checks, and an honest animation status.

SETUP
Work on a clear, firm surface. Near means toward your body; far means away from you. Left and right are your own left and right, not the camera’s.
The material front is called Side A and the reverse Side B. Their colors are optional visual aids, not the only way to follow the steps.

AUTHORS
fold-spec contributors — instructions, geometry

1. Place the square
Place the square with an edge nearest you and Side A facing up.
Orientation: Four edges form a square. The edge nearest you is the near edge.

2. Match the left and right edges
Lift the left edge and bring it onto the right edge. Align both pairs of corners before pressing the fold.
Animation not authored for this step.
Orientation: Keep the current near edge toward your body.
Check by touch: The two original outside edges lie together. The opposite long edge is a single folded spine.

3. Open the sheet
Lift the upper layer and return it to the left. Flatten gently; do not erase the center crease.
Animation not authored for this step.
Orientation: Keep the current near edge toward your body.
Check by touch: The sheet is square again with one crease across its center.

LIMITATIONS
Authored guidance has not been tested with blind folders or physically paper-folded as part of this release.
